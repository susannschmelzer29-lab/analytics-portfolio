Executive Summary hypothesis-driven · MECE · so-what

Situation. Management needs to know where sales come from, which levers move them, and where to act across 1,115 stores (1M+ store-days, 2013–2015).

Complication. Raw rankings and naive averages mislead — outliers inflate performance gaps, and holiday "uplifts" are largely a store-selection artefact.

Key findings MECE, with confidence

Recommendations prioritised

  1. Reallocate promo spend by store type & archetype — deploy the ~41 % (CI-backed) lever where uplift is largest, not uniformly. Owner: Category/Trade Marketing · Impact: high · Effort: low
  2. Run an archetype playbook — traffic tactics for frequency-driven, basket/cross-sell for basket-driven, turnaround review for the watchlist. Owner: Regional Ops · Impact: high · Effort: medium
  3. Benchmark within peer groups — compare each store to its archetype median, not the global ranking. Owner: Controlling · Impact: medium · Effort: low
  4. Seasonal & holiday planning on corrected effects — size capacity to the December/weekly curve and the within-store holiday reality. Owner: S&OP · Impact: medium · Effort: medium
  5. Upgrade location scoring — add location/frequency indicators beyond raw competition distance. Owner: Expansion · Impact: medium · Effort: medium

Every figure is reproducible from the notebook; effect sizes carry 95 % confidence intervals and segments are cross-checked by silhouette score.
