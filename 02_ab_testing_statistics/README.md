# 02 – A/B Testing & Experimentation

## 🎯 Business Problem
How can we determine whether a change in product or marketing improves conversion rates?

This project explores how businesses can validate decisions using statistical experimentation.

---

## 📊 Dataset
Simulated A/B test dataset.

Fields:
- group (A/B)
- conversion (0/1)
- user_id

---

## 🧠 Methods

- Hypothesis testing
- Conversion rate comparison
- Statistical significance
- Confidence intervals

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Small differences may not be statistically significant
- Sample size matters
- Business decisions require statistical validation

---

## 💡 Business Impact

- Reduce risky marketing decisions
- Avoid false positives
- Improve experimentation quality

---

## 🛠 Tools

- Python
- Statistical testing
